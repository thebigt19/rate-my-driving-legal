Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for a plate?
Open the Home tab, type a license plate number into the search bar, or tap “Snap a Plate” to detect a plate from a photo.

How do I write a review?
Open a driver profile and tap “Write a Review”. Choose thumbs up or thumbs down, add a comment, and optionally attach a photo.

How do I claim my plate?
On a plate’s profile, tap “Claim This Plate” and upload a photo of your registration document. An administrator will review your claim.

How do I report an inappropriate review?
Tap the flag icon on any review and select a reason.

How do I delete my account?
