Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 2026

Rate My Driving (“the App”) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out the standards we maintain to keep our community safe, in line with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy.

Our Commitment

  • We strictly prohibit any content, conduct, or communication that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers children.
  • We prohibit CSAM and any content that facilitates the abuse or exploitation of minors.
  • The App is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed to children.

Preventing and Combating CSAE

  • User reporting: Users can report or flag any content or user within the App. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Moderation: User-generated content (reviews, comments, and photos) is subject to moderation, and violating content is removed.
  • Enforcement: Accounts that violate these standards are suspended or permanently banned.
  • Reporting to authorities: We report apparent CSAM to the relevant national and regional authorities and hotlines (such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, NCMEC) as required by applicable law.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where the App is available. We regularly review these standards and our moderation practices.
